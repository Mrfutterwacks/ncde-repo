Chapter the First

Opening & Moving the Panel

Open Settings from the dock, or by name from NCDE Command. The window wears no Motif frame — it carries its own three jewels at the right of the title cartouche. minimises to the dock; maximises or restores; closes the panel.

Take hold of the cartouche itself — the gilt-edged title bar reading NCDE SETTINGS — and drag to move the window anywhere on the desk. Every change you make is written the moment you make it; there is no Save button to remember, and no Apply to forget.

A note on persistence

Settings take effect at once and survive a restart. Where a change touches the whole shell — a colour, a font, the glass — the desktop re-dresses itself live, without a logout.

The Three Wings

Appearance

Devices

System

Dock & Panels, Date & Time, Notifications, Session, and About — the running of the house.
If you remember the old panel

The former Accents and Glass tabs are gone — their work now lives, more fully, inside Filigree. Nothing was lost; it was gathered into one hand.

Chapter the Third

Filigree — the Look

The visual-design authority. One room governs colour, glass, and the colour of type.

Filigree begins with the colour wheel. Turn it and a five-tone palette is drawn beneath — Ground, Surface, Accent, Highlight, and Quill (the colour of text). The Base Calm dial quiets the grounds while leaving the accent bright, for a restrained desk that still keeps one vivid note.

  • Harmony offers single, complementary, triadic, or analogous schemes drawn from your chosen hue.
  • Contrast Guard checks, live, that Quill stays legible on Ground and Surface — a tick or a cross, by the WCAG measure.
  • In Context previews the palette over your real wallpaper, with a panel, a pane of glass, and a line of type.
  • Apply as Theme commits the palette to the whole shell; Lift current accent pulls the desk's present colour back into the wheel to adjust.

Glass by Surface

Panels, Dock, Widgets, and Menus each take their own Tint, Shine, Glow, and Border — or press Link all to accent to keep them of one cloth.

Widgets — Colour & Type

The Clock, Space, Weather, Stats, and Salon Nocturne each carry their own accent, text colour, and font, shown in a little live preview as you choose.

Font — Fill & Outline

Type is given both a fill and an outline, with an outline width — so a title stays readable on glass or over a busy wallpaper.

Palettes you favour may be named and saved — Filigree even suggests a name in the period manner. Saved palettes restore with a tap.

Chapter the Fourth

Wallpapers & Fonts

Wallpapers dresses the desk from a gallery, with a fit mode (fill, fit, centre, tile, or span), an optional per-monitor choice, and a slideshow that rotates a folder on an interval. Theme from Wallpaper samples the picture's dominant colour and hands it to Filigree, so the whole shell can breathe with the image behind it.

Fonts governs the families — an Interface face for titles and menus, a Document face for reading, and a Fixed-width face for terminals — with size scale, UI scale, hinting, and antialiasing. The colour of type is set next door, in Filigree.

Chapter the Fifth

Display

Arrange your screens by sight, name a primary, and set each one's resolution, refresh rate, scale, and orientation. Night Light warms the screen from sunset to sunrise — and because NCDE already knows where you are (see Chapter the Ninth), it needs no times from you; it simply follows the sun.

Chapter the Sixth

Input

Set the keyboard layout (a second may be added), with repeat delay and rate. For mouse and touchpad: pointer speed, natural scroll, tap-to-click, and disable-while-typing. The cursor takes a theme and a size — small, medium, or large.

Chapter the Seventh

Sound

Choose the output device and its volume and balance, the input device and its live level, and — below — a per-application mixer, so Salon Nocturne may sing while another app is hushed.

Chapter the Eighth

Power

Two columns — on battery and plugged in — set when the screen blanks and when the machine suspends (Never is allowed). Then the behaviours: what the lid does when closed, what the power button does when pressed, and whether the battery shows its percent.

Chapter the Ninth

Date & Time

The clock keeps itself. You should never set your timezone by hand.

NCDE finds where you are from the network — no satellite, no fuss — and sets the timezone for you. A small map shows a pin and the badge ✦ Located automatically. Travel to another city and the clock follows you; the zone updates itself when the network changes.

  • Hour format may be 12-hour, 24-hour, or Auto — which follows the region found for you.
  • Network time keeps the hour true to the second.
  • Region & Language are likewise found automatically, setting date order, units, and the first day of the week.
If you would rather choose

An escape hatch, “Set manually instead,” waits beneath the map for the traveller who wants a fixed zone. Choosing one turns the automatic locating off until you ask for it again.

ON YOUR PRIVACY

NCDE asks once, by a gentle toast, before it uses your network location — and it speaks only to set your clock. The result is kept, and looked up afresh only when your network changes.

Chapter the Tenth

Notifications

Silence the desk with Do Not Disturb, choose a corner for banners, and set Quiet Hours — a nightly span when only an alarm may speak. Each application keeps its own switch, so the Courier may call while the Command centre stays silent.

Chapter the Eleventh

Session

Autostart lists the programs that wake with you; add one, name its command, and switch it on or off. Default Applications names your browser, mail, files, and terminal. At the foot wait ⟲ Log Out and Lock, for the end of the day.

Chapter the Twelfth

About

The last room tells you the version of NCDE, the compositor it runs upon, and the particulars of your machine — its processor, memory, graphics, and disk. From here, too, this very Operating Manual may be opened again whenever you wish.
